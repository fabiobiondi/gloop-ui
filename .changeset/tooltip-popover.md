Add `Tooltip` and `Popover`: liquid bubbles with an arrow that drip out of the trigger. Tooltip opens on hover (with delay) or keyboard focus and accepts text or JSX. Popover holds forms and components and closes on outside click, Escape, from code (`close` render-prop, `usePopover()`, controlled `open`) or on rollout (`closeOnLeave`). Both flip and shift to stay inside the viewport.
