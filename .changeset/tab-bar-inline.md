`TabBar`: `layout="inline"` puts icon, label and value on one row, with tabs as wide as their content; `value` on each tab shows a counter or badge (next to the label inline, on the icon when stacked); `position="static"` keeps the bar in the page flow instead of fixed to the bottom. `GooPill` now animates its `length` with the same springs, so the pill stretches between items of different widths.
