New `GloopLoading`: a liquid loader where a drop falls in, bounces, swells into a blob and keeps dripping, with an optional `title` and `description`. `size`, `color` (solid or `[from, to]` gradient) and `speed` are configurable; `intro={false}` skips the fall. Static with `prefers-reduced-motion`.
