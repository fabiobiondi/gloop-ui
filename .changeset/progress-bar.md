New `ProgressBar`: a liquid progress bar whose blob head springs ahead on every update while the fill catches up, fused by the goo filter. Without `value` it is indeterminate, with blobs that run, merge and split. Optional `drip`, `showValue` (`'inside'` or `'bubble'`), `formatValue`, `color` (solid or `[from, to]` gradient), `thickness` and `speed`. Calm with `prefers-reduced-motion`.
